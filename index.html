📖 How to Use — Beverage Cart

Overview

Work through the tabs in order: Staff → Opening → POS → Restock (if needed) → Closing → Cashout. The step bar at the top is always visible — tap any step to jump to it.

1 · Staff — Start of Shift

1
Confirm your name, date and start time

Your name is pre-filled from your login. Make sure the date and time are correct.

2
Enter cash float received from Clubhouse

Enter the dollar amount of the cash float you received. This is tracked separately and returned to the manager at the end of your shift — it does not go into the safe envelope.

3
Check off your supplies

Confirm you have everything — Moneris machine, shooter cups, napkins, etc.

4
Tap "Next — Opening inventory →"

Saves your shift details and moves to the opening count.

2 · Opening — Count Your Stock

1
Count packaged goods

For each item, enter the quantity on hand using the +/− buttons, or tap the number to type it directly with the keypad.

2
Weigh liquor bottles

Place each bottle on a scale and enter the weight in grams in the opening weight field.

3
Tap "Confirm & lock opening stock"

Locks your counts and takes you straight to the POS to start taking orders. Opening counts cannot be changed after this point.

POS — Taking Orders

1
Tap "+ New Order"

Opens the order builder. Tap any item to add it. Tap again to increase the quantity. The number in the corner shows how many are left in stock.

Only items that were included in your opening count — plus anything added during a restock — will appear as available in the POS. If something isn't showing as available, it wasn't counted at opening or hasn't been restocked yet.
2
Tap "Charge" to take payment

Select Card, Cash, or Lucky Bucks. You can combine multiple payment methods on one order — just add each one and the balance updates automatically.

3
Card payment

A keypad appears. Enter the total amount shown on the Moneris terminal including any tip. The app calculates the tip for you.

4
Cash payment

Enter the amount received from the customer. Change given, tip, and amount applied to the bill are all calculated automatically.

5
Lucky Bucks

Tap + to enter the number of Lucky Bucks certificates. Each is worth $5.00. Lucky Bucks collected go into the cash envelope at the end of the shift.

6
Tap "Confirm payment"

Once the balance is $0.00, confirm the payment. The order is saved and you return to the POS home screen.

7
Editing an order

Tap "Edit order" on any completed order to change items or payment details. The original payment info is pre-loaded so you only change what's needed.

3 · Restock — Mid-Shift Top-Up

1
Tap "3 · Restock" in the step bar

Go here any time you pick up more stock from the Clubhouse during your shift.

2
Enter quantities being added

Enter only the new quantities you are adding — not your total on hand.

3
Tap "✓ Confirm restock"

Locks the restock round and returns you to the POS. You can do up to 10 restock rounds per shift.

⚠ Always tap Confirm restock — not "Skip to closing count". If you skip without confirming, those items won't be counted in your reconciliation and won't be available for sale in the POS module.

4 · Closing — End of Shift Count

1
Count remaining packaged goods

Enter the quantity of each item left at the end of your shift.

2
Weigh remaining liquor bottles

Weigh each bottle and enter the closing weight in grams. If a bottle is completely empty, check the "Empty" checkbox.

3
Tap "Calculate & cashout →"

Locks your closing counts and takes you to the Cashout tab with the full reconciliation calculated.

5 · Cashout — End of Shift Report

1
Enter total card payments from Moneris

Enter the total amount from your Moneris terminal report — the full amount charged including tips.

2
Review the reconciliation

The app calculates how much cash is due back to the club. If card and Lucky Bucks payments exceeded revenues, the balance will be paid out the following week through the tip-out process.

3
Prepare your cash envelope

Place the net cash amount (rounded to nearest dollar) plus any Lucky Bucks certificates into the safe envelope and drop it in the safe.

4
Return the cash float

Return the cash float to the manager separately — it does not go in the safe envelope.

5
Sign and send the report

Draw your signature in the box at the bottom of the page, then tap "Send report". The report is emailed automatically to management and to your registered email. You must sign before the Send button becomes active.
